01What VKT CleanMate does

Short version: VKT CleanMate is a self-service privacy tool for your own browser. It does not collect, transmit or sell your browsing data. Everything it knows stays on your device.

VKT CleanMate deletes browsing data (history, cache, cookies and other site data) that you ask it to delete, on a schedule or on demand. You control the scope: either only the domains on your clean list, or all domains except those on your protected list.

03Focus footprint is opt-in and minimal

  • The feature is off by default. Nothing is recorded until you turn it on.
  • By default it only records while the Footprint page is open. Close the page and recording stops.
  • It stores only the domain, the focused duration, the visit count and an hour-of-day histogram.
  • It does not store full URLs, page titles, search terms, screenshots or page content.
  • Data expires automatically after 30 days and can be wiped at any time with “Clear stats”.
  • The footprint is part of the paid tier; the CSV export lives on the same page.

04What we never do

  • ❌ No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.
  • ❌ No browsing data is ever uploaded to a server.
  • ❌ No data is sold or shared with third parties.

05Network requests

The only outbound request is licence verification, and only if you enter a licence key. It sends the licence key, a random install ID, the plugin ID and basic environment info (browser name/version, UI language, timezone) to api.annmax1983.com, at most once every two hours. It contains no browsing data.

07What VKT CleanMate cannot do

  • It cannot clear DNS caches, router logs, ISP records or server-side logs.
  • It cannot restore deleted history — the browser provides no API to write history back.
  • It cannot clean other browsers’ data, other profiles, or a synced copy on another device.
  • “On browser close” is best-effort: if the browser exits before the task finishes, cleanup is completed a few seconds after the next start.
  • It is not an anti-forensics or anti-surveillance tool, and it does not make you anonymous.

08Children

VKT CleanMate is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
