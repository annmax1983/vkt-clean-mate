01

What it actually does

Eight data types, five triggers, and a domain rule you can explain in one sentence.

01

Preview first — Every run from the popup starts with a summary: how many sites matched, how many history entries and cookies will go, and which retention window applies.

02

Targeted by default — Only the domains on your clean list are touched. Everything else is never included, so a free user has no way to lose data by accident.

03

Protected where it counts — In Full mode your protected domains are skipped entirely — banks, mail, anything you want to stay signed in to.

04

Eight data types — History, cache, cookies, downloads, form data, local storage, IndexedDB and service workers — each with its own switch; cache, cookies, local storage, IndexedDB and service workers clear per site, the other three browser-wide.

05

Five triggers — Browser startup, browser close, every N minutes, daily at a set time, after N minutes idle.

06

Per-trigger scope — Clear only history + cache every few hours, deep-clean when the browser closes.

Records the audio of one tab. Other tabs, system sounds and microphone input stay out of the file.

02

The tab keeps playing out loud while it records, so you can follow what you are capturing.

03

Constant memory: audio is encoded in one-second blocks and written to disk as it arrives, so length is limited by free disk space, not RAM.

04

MP3 at 128 or 64 kbps, or uncompressed WAV when the samples have to stay untouched.

05

Auto-save: the finished file downloads to Downloads/TabTape/ and is kept in the built-in library.

06

Crash-safe: if the browser closes mid-recording, the library offers to recover what already reached disk.

Also included

  • Retention window — Keep the last 1, 3, 7 or 30 days — or everything.
  • Focus footprint — Time actually spent watching per domain, not tabs opened. Off by default; free records only while its page is open, Pro keeps recording in the background.
  • Shortcuts — Alt+Shift+C cleans now. Alt+Shift+X asks first, then cleans and closes every tab except one. Both work with auto-clean off.
  • It cannot clear DNS caches, router logs, ISP records or server-side logs.
  • It cannot restore deleted history — browsers provide no API to write history back.
  • It cannot clean other browsers’ data, other profiles, or a synced copy on another device.
  • “On browser close” is best-effort: if the browser exits mid-task, cleanup finishes a few seconds after the next start.
  • It cannot limit history, downloads, form data or passwords to a single site — the browser doesn’t expose that, and the UI says so.
  • It is not an anonymity or anti-surveillance tool. It is a self-service privacy tool for your own browser.
  • Trim without re-encoding (Pro): WAV is cut at byte offsets, MP3 at frame boundaries, so the export is the same audio, just shorter.
  • Library: play, download, rename and delete recordings, see storage used, and find them by the source page title and URL.
  • Keyboard control: Alt+Shift+R starts and stops.
  • Six interface languages: English, 中文, 日本語, Deutsch, Español, Français.
  • Free covers 20 minutes per recording. Nothing expires, no feature is taken away, and your files carry no watermark.

02

Free vs Pro

The licence buys one thing: the full wipe. The protected list, the footprint data and the CSV export are free.

Free

Targeted mode (clean list, unlimited) · Manual + scheduled cleaning · Preview on manual runs · Protected list, in both modes · Focus footprint, ranking, suggestions · Footprint CSV export

Pro

Full mode (all sites minus protected) · Recording after the footprint page closes · 30-day trend, this week vs last

Free

up to 20 minutes per file, MP3 or WAV, auto-download, the full library (play, download, rename, delete), recovery of unfinished recordings, and the keyboard shortcut.

Pro

no time limit — a recording runs until you stop it or the tab closes — plus lossless trim. One licence key activates on two browsers, and removing the licence frees that slot again.

One licence covers two browsers, and the free features keep working if it expires. Nothing is removed until the preview says so — scheduled runs clean on their own.

Private by design

Your browsing data never leaves the device. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry — the only outbound request is licence validation, and only if you enter a key.
